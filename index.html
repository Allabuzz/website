Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making our website usable for everyone.

Last updated: January 2026

Our Commitment

All Abuzz Digital is committed to making our website accessible to all visitors, including people with disabilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and continuously work to improve the user experience for everyone.

What We're Doing

Our accessibility efforts include:

  • Color contrast: Maintaining sufficient contrast ratios between text and backgrounds throughout the site
  • Keyboard navigation: Ensuring all interactive elements are navigable and operable via keyboard
  • Screen reader support: Using semantic HTML, ARIA attributes, and descriptive labels to support assistive technologies
  • Alt text: Providing meaningful alternative text for images and visual content
  • Responsive design: Building layouts that adapt to different screen sizes, zoom levels, and devices
  • Clear structure: Using proper heading hierarchy, landmarks, and consistent navigation patterns

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas of the site may not yet fully meet all WCAG 2.1 AA criteria. We are actively working to identify and resolve any issues. If you encounter a barrier, please let us know.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you experience any difficulty accessing content or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at hello@allabuzzdigital.com. We take all feedback seriously and will do our best to respond within 5 business days.

A Note for Our Clients: Why Accessibility Matters for Your Business

If you're a small business owner — especially in mental health, healthcare, or any client-facing industry — website accessibility isn't just a nice-to-have. It's increasingly a legal requirement. The ADA applies to business websites, and lawsuits over inaccessible sites have risen sharply in recent years.

More importantly, accessibility is about making sure every potential client can find and use your services. People with visual impairments, motor disabilities, cognitive differences, and other challenges deserve the same access to your business as anyone else.

When we build websites for our clients, we incorporate accessibility best practices from the start — color contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader support, and semantic structure. It's the right thing to do, and it protects your business.
